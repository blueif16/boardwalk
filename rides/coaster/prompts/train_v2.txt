Image 1 is a close-up of our storybook painting of the Giant Dipper, a white wooden roller coaster with dark red track. Images 2 and 3 are our style frame and its tiny animal characters. Draw a sprite sheet of the Giant Dipper's train in exactly this painting's style: thin warm dark-brown outlines (#2c201b) around every shape, thinner tinted lines inside, flat warm pastel fills with one soft shade tone, chunky rounded toy-like proportions, a gentle paper texture.

The train must stand out clearly against the red track it runs on, so its cars are NOT red: three small open roller coaster cars in the classic 1920s wooden-coaster style, each with a low rounded body painted soft cornflower blue (#85adcd), a thick cream trim along its top edge, one thin butter-yellow stripe along the side, and two small dark wheels showing below. The front car has a rounded nose with a small round brass headlamp. Two cute animal riders sit in each car, side by side as seen in the views below: a bear and a bunny in the front car, a cat and a dog in the middle car, a fox and a panda in the rear car, in the style of image 3. The riders are big for the car, like toys: their whole heads and shoulders show above the car's side, both arms up, cheerful open-mouthed faces, plain colourful t-shirts.

Layout, strict: on a plain flat pure white background, a grid of 3 columns and 3 rows. Each cell holds exactly one car with its riders, whole, centred, all cars the same size, with a wide empty margin; nothing touches a neighbour or the edge.

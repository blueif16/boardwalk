Image 1 is a close-up of our storybook painting of the Giant Dipper, a white wooden roller coaster with dark red track. Images 2 and 3 are our style frame and its tiny animal characters. Draw a sprite sheet of the Giant Dipper's train in exactly this painting's style: thin warm dark-brown outlines (#2c201b) around every shape, thinner tinted lines inside, flat warm pastel fills with one soft shade tone, chunky rounded toy-like proportions, a gentle paper texture.

The train: three small open roller coaster cars in the classic 1920s wooden-coaster style. Each car has a low rounded red body (the same red as the track in image 1), a cream trim along its top edge, a thin gold stripe along the side, two small dark wheels showing below. The front car has a rounded nose with a small round brass headlamp. Two cute tiny animal riders sit in each car, one behind the other (a bear, a bunny, a cat, a dog, a fox and a panda, in the style of image 3), only their heads and shoulders above the car's side, some with both arms up, cheerful faces.

Layout, strict: on a plain flat pure white background, a grid of 3 columns and 3 rows. Each cell holds exactly one car, whole, centred, all cars the same size, with a wide empty margin; nothing touches a neighbour or the edge.

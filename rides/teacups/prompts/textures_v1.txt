Image 1 is our style frame: a cute storybook map of a seaside boardwalk, with thin tinted outlines, flat warm pastel fills, one soft shade tone and a gentle watercolour paper texture. Draw a TEXTURE SHEET in exactly that style, for wrapping around small toy teacups in a 3D model.

Layout, strict: seven flat horizontal bands stacked from top to bottom, each filling the full width of the image, all the same height, separated by thin plain white gaps. Each band is a flat, front-on, evenly lit pattern swatch (not a drawing of a cup, no perspective, no shadows, no objects), the same pattern repeated evenly from the left edge to the right edge. Along the top of every band runs a narrow cream rim stripe edged with a thin warm gold line.

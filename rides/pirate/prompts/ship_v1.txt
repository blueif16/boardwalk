No mast and no sails.
The view: from the side, the ship's long axis exactly horizontal, seen a little from above, so the inside of the ship shows: six rows of empty wooden benches running across the ship, stepping up towards the bow and the stern. The near side of the hull hides the lower half of the benches.
It hangs from two straight steel arms, blue with red bands, that rise from the middle of the hull, lean together and meet at one small round hub at the top centre, directly above the middle of the ship. The hub is above the ship's deck by about two thirds of the ship's length.

No frame, no legs, no ground, no shadow, no people, no animals, no text. The background is flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, also between the arms and between the benches. The whole ship and its arms are centred with a wide empty margin and nothing touches the edges.

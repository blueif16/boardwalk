Image 1 is a close-up of our storybook painting of Logger's Revenge, a log flume ride: a wooden mill house, wooden water channels and a long water chute. Images 2 and 3 are our style frame and its tiny animal characters. Draw a sprite sheet of the flume's log boats in exactly this painting's style: thin warm dark-brown outlines (#2c201b) around every shape, thinner tinted lines inside, flat warm pastel fills with one soft shade tone, chunky rounded toy-like proportions, a gentle paper texture.

The logs must stand out clearly against light-blue water and teal water chutes, so they are warm honey-brown wood, not blue: three hollowed-out log boats, each a fat rounded log carved open on top like a little canoe, with visible bark texture and a lighter cream-wood rim around the opening, and a rounded front end with the log's cut rings showing. Riders sit in a row inside each log, as in the views below: a panda, a bunny and a fox in the first log; a duck, a tiger and a koala in the second log; a pig, a raccoon and a frog in the third log, in the style of image 3. The riders are big for the log, like toys: their whole heads and shoulders show above the rim, the front rider holds both arms up, cheerful open-mouthed faces, plain colourful t-shirts.

Layout, strict: on a plain flat pure white background, a grid of 3 columns and 3 rows. Each cell holds exactly one log with its riders, whole, centred, all logs the same size, with a wide empty margin; nothing touches a neighbour or the edge.

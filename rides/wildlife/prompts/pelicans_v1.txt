Layout, strict: a grid of 5 columns and 3 rows on a plain flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background with no shading or texture. One pelican per cell, whole, centred, with a wide empty margin; nothing touches a neighbour or the edge of its cell. No shadows, no water, no splashes, no fish, no ground, no grid lines, no text, no labels.

The new time: golden hour, about forty minutes before sunset. A low warm sun from the lower left bathes everything in soft peach and honey-gold light; the sides of things that face the sun glow warm, the sides away from it turn a soft lilac. Keep it a storybook painting: the pastel colours stay clean and bright, only warmer. The sand is warm apricot, the boardwalk planks honey, the white coaster lattice peach-gold with lilac shade, the street and the parking lots warm lilac-grey, the town's walls glowing peach.

Every artificial light is switched off.

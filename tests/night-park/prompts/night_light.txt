The new time: a clear moonlit night, about an hour after sunset. A soft deep-blue night light covers everything evenly. Keep it a storybook painting: the pastel colours stay readable and gentle, only cooler and darker, never grey, muddy or black, and the thin warm outlines stay visible. The sand is a soft pale lavender-blue, lighter than the boardwalk; the boardwalk planks are dusky mauve-brown; the white coaster lattice is pale periwinkle; the red track a deep cranberry; palms and planting deep blue-green; the street and the parking lots dark slate blue; the town's walls muted blue-lilac under dark roofs.

Every artificial light is switched off: the lamp globes are pale unlit glass with no halo and no pool of light, every window is dark, the stalls, signs and rides are unlit. No warm glow anywhere.

The new time: twilight, about twenty minutes after sunset, the blue hour. The sun has gone down beyond the left edge; its afterglow from the lower left touches the tops and the left-facing sides of things with soft rose and peach, and everything else sits in periwinkle and violet shade. Keep it a storybook painting: darker and cooler than daytime but much lighter than night, the pastel colours still clean and rich, never grey or muddy. The sand is mauve-pink fading to periwinkle, the boardwalk planks dusty rose, the white coaster lattice lilac with a pink rim of light, the street and the parking lots slate violet, the town's walls lilac and rose.

Every artificial light is still switched off: lamps, windows, stalls, signs and rides are all unlit.

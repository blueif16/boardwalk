The new time: early morning, just after sunrise. A soft, fresh light: pale rose and apricot where the low sun from the right touches the tops and the right-facing sides of things, cool lilac-blue in the shade, a faint morning haze over the town. Keep it a storybook painting: the pastel colours stay clean and gentle, a little cooler and paler than midday. The sand is pale peach with a lilac tint, the boardwalk planks soft rose-brown, the white coaster lattice rosy on its sunlit side and lilac on the rest, the street and the parking lots cool grey-lilac.

Every artificial light is switched off.
